### 🔬 About me

I build **AI that detects early signs of Alzheimer's disease from everyday speech**, and the **hardware that lets it run privately on small, low-power devices** such as FPGAs and embedded GPUs instead of the cloud.

- 🎓 Ph.D. in Electrical & Computer Engineering at **Michigan State University** (2025 – present, GPA 4.00)
- 🧠 Working on: speech biomarkers, cognitive assessment and edge-deployable models for Alzheimer's detection
- ⚡ Background: analog and RF IC design (VCOs, charge pumps, PLLs in 28 nm CMOS) at **IIT Ropar** and **Chipex Technologies**
- 🏅 Open Scholarship Fellowship, MSU (2026) · 3 patent applications on LC and quadrature VCOs

### 🧪 Projects

- **Edge AI for speech-based Alzheimer's detection.** INT8-quantized speech models deployed on Xilinx Kria KV260 (Vitis-AI) and NVIDIA Jetson (TensorRT).
